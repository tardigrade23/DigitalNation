Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Digital Nation is an idle strategy game about building a digital state. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it. The game is currently a prototype.

You can play without an account

Without signing in, your progress is stored only in your browser on your device. We do not receive it.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email addressWhen you sign inTo send your one-time sign-in code and identify your account
Game save: nation name, leader name, progress, settingsWhen you are signed inTo keep your progress the same on all your devices
Device type (for example "Android" or "Computer")When you are signed inTo show where your last save came from
Gems balance and a record of gems earned and spentWhen you are signed inTo keep your gems and purchases safe and prevent cheating
Leaderboard entry: nation name, leader name, flag and scoreWhen you are signed inTo show the world leaderboard. Other players can see this entry
Anonymous gameplay statistics, such as "finished the tutorial" or "reached a new era"Only if you allow itTo improve game balance. They are linked to a random ID, not your email

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts or location. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Where data is stored

Account and game data are stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. Encyclopedia pictures are served from our own site; they come from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses.

Purchases and ads

In this prototype, purchases and ads are simulated: no money is charged and no ad network is used. If real payments or ads are added, this policy will be updated before they go live, and payments will be handled by Google Play or the App Store.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Anonymous statistics are kept for up to 12 months.

Delete your account

In the game, open Profile → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, cloud save, gems record and leaderboard entry. Progress stored on your device is not affected; use Reset progress on this device to remove it.

If you cannot sign in anymore, ask for deletion at github.com/tardigrade23/DigitalNation/issues, and include the email address of the account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can turn anonymous statistics off at any time in Profile → Settings. If you live in the EU or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
